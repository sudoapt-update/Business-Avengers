Questions

About who it is for, your data and the demo.

Who is FinPath for?

People in their first years of running a business: online shops, marketplace sellers, market stalls. No finance background needed.

Is the sample store real?

No. The demo opens with a sample store, Emberline Candle Co., and 120 days of invented orders. Every figure is calculated from those orders.

Do I connect my store?

Not in the demo. Store connections are simulated and no real account is accessed.

Does FinPath replace my accountant?

No. FinPath shows where your money goes each day; your accountant still files your taxes. Nothing here is financial or tax advice.

Where does FinPath keep my data?

In your browser. Only an adviser question leaves it, sent with the sample store's figures to the AI model.
